ステージ1 コース概要

  1. 1. Hello!5 min

    Respond to a greeting and introduce yourself at a community class welcome desk

    hello · I · you

    文法スキル: I am; Are you ...?
  2. 2. He, she, and a friend5 min

    Introduce Rafi and Lina as friends at the community class welcome-circle photo wall

    he · she · friend

    文法スキル: He/She is ...; He/She is my friend.
  3. 3. What is this?5 min

    Return to the community class welcome table to ask about a nearby book and farther-away water

    this · that · what · book · water

    文法スキル: What is this/that? Answer: This is a book. / That is water.
  4. 4. I have two6 min

    Receive class books at the community class welcome table, count from one to two, and say what you have

    have · one · two · book · books

    文法スキル: I have one book; I have two books
  5. 5. I like it5 min

    Answer yes or no about familiar books and water at the community class preference board

    like · don't · yes · no

    文法スキル: Yes, I like ...; No, I don't like ...
  6. 6. Eat and drink6 min

    Choose water or bread at the community class refreshment table and say what you want to drink or eat

    want · drink · eat · bread

    文法スキル: I want to drink water; I want to eat bread
  7. 7. Come and go6 min

    Understand basic movement

    come · go · home

    文法スキル: Go home without to
  8. 8. Where is it?6 min

    Ask about and understand location

    where · here · there

    文法スキル: Question order with where + is
  9. 9. It is — or it isn't6 min

    Build basic affirmation and negation

    is · not · isn't

    文法スキル: Be + not; the contraction isn't
  10. 10. Maya's morning7 min

    Bring everything back in a mini story

    morning · then · home

    文法スキル: Initial time phrases and then
