Linguletbahasa Inggris · Tahap 1

Kursus interaktif bahasa Inggris gratis

Mulai belajar bahasa Inggris dengan pelajaran pertama lima menit—tanpa perlu masuk. Lihat jalur Tahap 1 dengan 10 unit.

Mulai pelajaran pertama gratis

Selesaikan enam cek dan jawaban ketik mandiri di Unit 1, lalu masuk untuk menyimpan bukti kemampuan terverifikasi.

Silabus Tahap 1

  1. 1. Hello!

    5 menit

    Respond to a greeting and introduce yourself at a community class welcome desk

    hello · I · you

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: I am; Are you ...?

  2. 2. He, she, and a friend

    5 menit

    Introduce Rafi and Lina as friends at the community class welcome-circle photo wall

    he · she · friend

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: He/She is ...; He/She is my friend.

  3. 3. What is this?

    5 menit

    Return to the community class welcome table to ask about a nearby book and farther-away water

    this · that · what · book · water

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: What is this/that? Answer: This is a book. / That is water.

  4. 4. I have two

    6 menit

    Receive class books at the community class welcome table, count from one to two, and say what you have

    have · one · two · book · books

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: I have one book; I have two books

  5. 5. I like it

    5 menit

    Answer yes or no about familiar books and water at the community class preference board

    like · don't · yes · no

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: Yes, I like ...; No, I don't like ...

  6. 6. Eat and drink

    6 menit

    Choose water or bread at the community class refreshment table and say what you want to drink or eat

    want · drink · eat · bread

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: I want to drink water; I want to eat bread

  7. 7. Come and go

    6 menit

    Follow the daily route into community English class and home after class

    come · go · home · class

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: I come to class; I go home

  8. 8. Where is it?

    6 menit

    Ask and find the book beside Maya and the water farther away on the community English classroom location board

    where · here · there

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: Where is ...?; It is here or there

  9. 9. It is — or it isn't

    6 menit

    Check the wrong water label and correct book label on the same object in community English class

    is · not · isn't

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: Is for positive; is not or isn't for negative

  10. 10. Maya's morning

    7 menit

    Tell Maya's three-part community English class story: come to class, drink water, then go home

    morning · then

    Kemampuan tata bahasa: Morning sets the start; then introduces what follows
