Stage 1 course outline

  1. 1. Halo!

    5 min

    Greet someone and meet saya and kamu

    halo · saya · kamu

    Grammar capability: Sentences without a linking verb

  2. 2. Dia teman saya

    5 min

    Introduce names and friends

    dia · teman · nama

    Grammar capability: Noun + possessor; gender-neutral dia

  3. 3. Ini apa?

    5 min

    Name common things around you

    ini · itu · apa

    Grammar capability: Demonstrative position; question word apa

  4. 4. Saya punya dua

    6 min

    Express possession and basic quantity

    punya · satu · dua

    Grammar capability: Number + noun; no plural change

  5. 5. Saya suka

    5 min

    Share likes and simple responses

    suka · tidak · ya

    Grammar capability: Negating verbs with tidak

  6. 6. Makan dan minum

    6 min

    Express essential everyday needs

    makan · minum · mau

    Grammar capability: Unchanged verbs; mau + verb

  7. 7. Datang dan pergi

    6 min

    Understand basic movement

    datang · pergi · pulang

    Grammar capability: Pulang for going home; datang and viewpoint

  8. 8. Di mana?

    6 min

    Ask about and understand location

    di · mana · sini

    Grammar capability: Location di versus direction ke

  9. 9. Tidak atau bukan?

    6 min

    Distinguish action and identity negation

    tidak · bukan · benar

    Grammar capability: Tidak versus bukan

  10. 10. Pagi Maya

    7 min

    Bring everything back in a mini story

    pagi · kemudian · rumah

    Grammar capability: Time words and integrated word order
