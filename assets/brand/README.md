# Lingulet logo assets

The mark belongs to the Integ.Life logo family: the master leaf remains intact,
while the lower-right badge integrates Lingulet's warm black field, white `L`,
and vermilion page-turn tab.

## Palette

- Family blue: `#274b62`
- Ink: `#25221d`
- Vermilion: `#b43b24`
- Paper: `#fffdf7`
- Family paper: `#f1eee6`

## Files and usage

- `lingulet-logo.svg`: canonical scalable mark for MCP connections, website UI,
  and general brand use.
- `lingulet-logo-maskable.svg`: maskable-safe copy of the canonical family mark.
- `png/lingulet-logo-{16,32,48,64,128,192,256,512}.png`: transparent PNG exports.
- `chrome-extension/icons/icon-{16,32,48,128}.png`: Chrome Extension icons.

Keep the master leaf, badge position, proportions, and colors unchanged.
